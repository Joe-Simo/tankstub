Logging a fill-up

Tap + and enter the odometer reading, the amount of fuel, and the total you paid. Leave Filled the tank on when you filled to the top, and turn it off for a partial fill.

How economy is measured

Economy is measured from one full tank to the next. A partial fill adds its fuel to the next full tank, so your number stays accurate. If you forgot to log a fill-up, turn on Missed logging the fill-up before this on the next one, so that tank is left out instead of counted wrong. Your first full tank sets the starting point, so economy appears after your second one.

Units

Choose miles or kilometers, gallons or liters, and mpg, mpg (UK), L/100 km, or km/L for each vehicle under Settings, then Vehicles and Units. Changing distance or fuel units does not convert readings you already logged. The economy unit can change any time.

Importing and exporting

In Settings, Export Fill-Ups as CSV creates a spreadsheet file you can save or share. Import reads a CSV with a date, an odometer reading or trip distance, a fuel amount, and a total cost or price per unit, and adds the rows to the current vehicle. Rows already in your log are skipped.

Service reminders

With Tankstub Pro, add reminders for oil changes, tire rotations, or registration by date, by odometer reading, or both. Date reminders send a notification at 9:00 AM that day. Odometer reminders show as Due once a fill-up reaches that reading. If notifications don't arrive, open Settings on your iPhone, then Notifications, then Tankstub, and turn on Allow Notifications.

Widgets

With Tankstub Pro, touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Tankstub to see your average economy, last tank, and this month's fuel spend.

Purchases

Tankstub Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If Pro isn't unlocked after buying, open Settings in Tankstub and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple Account. For refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Your data

Tankstub keeps your vehicles, fill-ups, and reminders only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data, so export a CSV first if you want a copy. See the Privacy Policy.
