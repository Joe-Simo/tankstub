Tankstub

Privacy Policy

Tankstub does not collect any data. Your fuel log stays on your device.

Effective October 27, 2026.

What Tankstub stores

Your vehicles, fill-ups (odometer readings, fuel amounts, prices, notes), and service reminders are stored only on your device, in an App Group shared with Tankstub's widgets. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What Tankstub does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • No third-party SDKs on iPhone. On Android, the only one is Google Play Billing, used for the Pro purchase.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access. Tankstub does not track your trips or where you buy fuel.

CSV import and export

If you export a CSV, Tankstub creates a file on your device and hands it to the share sheet. Where it goes after that is your choice. If you import a CSV, Tankstub reads the file you pick on your device and adds its rows to your log.

Purchases

Tankstub Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email, or payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your device so it can unlock Pro features. Apple's handling of purchase information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Widgets and reminders

These features run on your device using Apple's system frameworks. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Tankstub does not send their data anywhere.

Backups and deleting your data

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device backup may include Tankstub's data, under Apple's terms. To delete everything Tankstub stores, delete the app from your iPhone.

Tankstub for Android

Tankstub for Android works the same way. Your vehicles, fill-ups, reminders, and settings are stored in one file inside the app on your phone or tablet. Tankstub has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, does not use your location, and does not send your data to us or anyone else.

Tankstub Pro is bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; Tankstub only asks Google Play whether you own Pro. The Google Play Billing library that makes this work may send diagnostic data about the purchase flow to Google.

Service reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. CSV export goes only where you choose to share it, and CSV import reads only the file you pick.

If you turn on Android backup, your Tankstub file can be included in your Google backup, which you control and which we cannot read. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device.

Children

Tankstub does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
