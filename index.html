Tankstub

Your real fuel economy, tank to tank.

Log a fill-up in seconds: odometer, fuel, and what you paid. Tankstub works out your economy from one full tank to the next, and what each mile or kilometer costs you.

One vehicle with unlimited fill-ups is free with no time limit. Tankstub Pro is a one-time purchase that adds more vehicles, service reminders, widgets, and monthly and yearly cost reports. No account. No ads. No tracking. Your log stays on your iPhone.
